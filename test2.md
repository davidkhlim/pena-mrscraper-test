Keep them **casual, not scripted**—drop them naturally during answers.

# 🔥 1. Value-focused (very strong)

> “I usually try to look beyond just the data itself—more on how the client is actually using it, because that’s where we can identify opportunities to improve or expand the solution.”

👉 Use when talking about:

* client needs
* upsell
* product thinking

---

# 🔥 2. Delivery → Growth connection (CEO signal)

> “From my experience, when delivery is consistent and expectations are clear, it becomes much easier to have conversations around expanding scope or improving the solution.”

👉 This subtly says:

* you understand trust → revenue
* you’re not “selling”, you’re building

---

# 🔥 3. Product maturity thinking (very PM-level)

> “I think a lot of recurring issues come from expectation gaps, so I try to structure things early—like defining what ‘correct data’ means—so we reduce repeated back-and-forth later.”

👉 Use when discussing:

* your test
* process improvement
* client complaints

---

# 🧠 How to use them (important)

* Don’t dump all 3 at once
* Use **1 per relevant answer**
* Say them like you're thinking out loud, not presenting

---

# 🚀 Bonus (if you want one slightly bolder line)

> “For me, scraping is just the foundation—the real value is how the data fits into the client’s workflow.”

---

These lines are subtle, but they signal:

* senior thinking
* product awareness
* client + business mindset

Exactly what they’re looking for.
